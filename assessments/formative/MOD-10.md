# MOD-10 Improvement Evaluation Promotion and Rollback

This read-only self-check awards no status and is not assessment or runtime evidence.

This formative, read-only reflection checks whether you can convert a weakness into a reversible candidate without changing anything. An agent omitted mandatory source IDs, reflected on the failure, and proposed both a memory patch and a higher retry limit. Explain how to classify the observation and change surfaces, preserve the baseline, split candidates, freeze cases and rubric, separate roles, and define rejection, quarantine, promotion, monitoring, or rollback decisions.

## Must-pass invariants

1. Keep observation, candidate, proposal, evaluation, promotion, rejection, quarantine, monitoring, and rollback distinct; persistence, compilation, self-critique, or later success is not evidence of improvement by itself.
2. Freeze baseline, cases, rubric, mandatory gates, environment, budgets, and stop conditions before comparison, and preserve exact candidate identity, provenance, raw evidence, regressions, and uncertainty.
3. Separate subject, observer, proposer, evaluator, promoter, monitor, rollback owner, and supervisor authority; the proposer cannot be sole evaluator or promoter, and controller policy remains outside model-editable memory.

## Strong vs. weak response

**Strong:** Records the missing-source event as bounded evidence, separates the memory candidate from the controller retry-policy candidate, identifies reconstructable baselines and rollback targets, freezes transfer and regression cases, quarantines conflicted or exposed evaluation, requires critical gates before quality averages, and limits any later recommendation to a named scope with independent authority and explicit monitoring and rollback triggers.

**Weak:** “Commit both fixes, rerun the same prompt, and promote globally if the answer improves.”

## Misconception indicators

- A commit hash is a quality score.
- Monitoring substitutes for pre-promotion evaluation.
- An evaluator’s pass activates a candidate.
- Rollback erases external consequences.

## Self-check

**Continue** if all invariants are explicit and every verdict states its evidence and authority boundary. **Revisit** if cases change after results, surfaces remain bundled, or persistence is treated as promotion.
