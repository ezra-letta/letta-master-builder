This read-only self-check awards no status and is not assessment or runtime evidence.

Describe how a separately authorized offline practicum should evaluate unfamiliar provisioning, recovery, uncertain-effect, and supervised-improvement scenarios without confusing exact-package compilation, project-fixture conformance, independent evaluator judgment, integrity review, or operational evidence. ## Must-pass invariants

1. Machine gates, independent evaluator judgment, and integrity review retain separate evidence, identities, scopes, and status authority; the learner cannot self-award or appoint itself.
2. Any critical noncompensable failure—including blind retry after uncertainty, authority loss continuation, forbidden effect, topology-parity inference, unusable rollback, or contaminated independence—fails the attempt without averaging.
3. Every artifact and decision is bound to exact identity, provenance, digest, attempt conditions, and claim scope; synthetic or static evidence cannot be relabeled as another evidence class.

## Strong vs. weak response

**Strong:** “Compilation can establish compatibility with locked declarations, fixture gates can establish offline conformance, and an independent evaluator can judge architecture and transfer only for the digested attempt; integrity remains separately reviewed.”

**Weak:** “If all tests pass, award Master Builder and approve deployment.”

## Misconception indicators

Flag memorized-fixture success called transfer, withheld perturbations exposed through coaching, matching filenames substituted for digests, high averages offsetting critical failures, or publication and turn success treated as improvement proof.
